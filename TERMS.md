## 1. About the service

IronPulse is a gym-management web application built as a **student-grade project**. It lets gym owners list and
verify branches, sell memberships and products, manage staff and payroll, and lets members find gyms, follow
AI-assisted workout plans, train with a live voice coach and chat with trainers. By creating an account or using
the site you agree to these Terms. If you don't agree, please don't use IronPulse.

Gyms listed on IronPulse are independent businesses. Each gym owner is solely responsible for their premises,
equipment, staff, prices, classes, shop products and the services they provide. IronPulse does not operate any gym
or shop.

## 2. Accounts & sign-in

- You must give accurate information and keep your password secret. You're responsible for activity on your account.
- One person, one account: an e-mail address, a phone number and a username can each belong to only one account.
- You can sign in with your e-mail, your **@username**, your phone number or your **member ID** (for example
  IP-000123), plus your password. Forgot something? Use "Forgot password" or "Forgot username" on the sign-in page -
  we e-mail the details only to the address on the account.
- Every account except the platform administrator must upload a government ID card. Gym owners and staff must also
  provide a clear photo of themselves. Staff are approved only after they create and link their own account.
- We may suspend or close accounts that break these Terms, that appear fraudulent, or that put others at risk.

## 3. Minors & guardians

Users under 18 **need the consent of a parent or legal guardian**, whose contact details are required at sign-up.
The guardian receives a copy of every notification and e-mail sent to the minor. Guardians are responsible for
supervising the minor's training, purchases and use of IronPulse. Products marked 18+ (for example some
supplements) cannot be bought by minors.

## 4. Health & safety

Exercise carries a risk of injury. **Consult a doctor before starting any training programme**, especially if you
have a health condition, an injury, are pregnant or take medication. Stop immediately and seek medical help if you
feel pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or shortness of breath.

IronPulse can flag unusual entries - for example a sudden change in the weight or height you log, or symptoms you
report in a wellness check or to the voice coach - and may pause your gym activities until a gym admin reviews
them. IronPulse does not measure your heart rate or blood pressure. These checks are simple rules and AI
estimates: they can miss problems and they can raise false alarms. They are **not a diagnosis** and do not replace
a healthcare professional. **In an emergency, call your local emergency services.**

## 5. AI features

- **AI is available only to signed-in users.** Guests can browse, but AI answers, plans and advice require an account.
- **AI can make mistakes.** Answers, workout plans, plan and product advice, summaries and suggestions are **for
  information only** and may be wrong, incomplete or unsuitable for you. Always double-check important information.
- Models are tried in order - Gemma via a gateway, Gemma in a local Docker container, then Claude - and when all
  are unavailable the app tells you. Non-AI features (pre-recorded videos, chat, bookings, rule-based safety
  checks) keep working.
- Safety- and money-related AI output is re-checked by deterministic rules, but you remain responsible for your
  decisions. To answer quickly, identical questions may get a recently cached answer.
- Text and images you send to AI features may be processed by those providers to produce an answer. Don't include
  information you don't want processed.

## 6. Live voice coach

- The live coach talks with you during a workout. **Speech recognition is done by your browser's speech service**
  (for example Google in Chrome or Apple in Safari) under that provider's terms. IronPulse never records or stores
  your audio. You can turn the microphone off at any time and type instead.
- The coach's voice is generated by a self-hosted text-to-speech engine (Kokoro) or, if that is unavailable, by your
  device's built-in voice.
- You can ask the coach to pause, quit, change the exercise or the intensity. If you report chest pain, trouble
  breathing, fainting or similar symptoms, the session stops immediately, emergency guidance is shown, your gym
  activities may be paused and your gym (and guardian, for minors) may be notified. **The coach is not a medical
  service - call emergency services if you feel unwell.**
- The animated face and its emotions are a visual aid; they don't reflect any assessment of you.

## 7. Protective gear suggestions

Based on the conditions you record, IronPulse may suggest protective gear - for example a back support strap or
lifting belt for back problems, gloves for hand, grip or skin problems, or wrist wraps and knee sleeves. These are
**general suggestions, not medical prescriptions**. Some gear isn't suitable for every condition (for example
lifting belts with a hernia or during pregnancy). Ask your doctor or physiotherapist, and follow the manufacturer's
instructions.

## 8. Verification & ID cards

Branches, staff documents and ID cards are checked automatically by several OCR engines and AI, with manual review
when needed. A "verified" badge means the documents passed these checks at that time - it is not a guarantee of
safety, quality, licensing or legal compliance. ID card images are **encrypted at rest** and shown only through
authorised pages; ID numbers are stored masked. Branches are listed only after verification and with at least 3
photos.

## 9. Memberships & the 30 km rule

- Gyms can offer up to 12 plan types (offline, online or combo, each optionally with a trainer and/or the AI
  coach). Pre-recorded videos are included in every plan. Gym owners can disable plans or change prices at any
  time; existing memberships keep the price you paid.
- Offline and combo plans are only sold when a published branch is within **30 km** of you (by GPS, or your city as
  a fallback). If you're farther away, please visit the gym owner directly for offline registration.
- Offline purchases are completed **at the branch desk**: a one-time code (10 minutes, limited attempts) is sent to
  the gym owner and entered by you. When you share GPS we also check you are within 1.5 km of the branch.
- Membership refunds, freezes and cancellations are set by each gym. Contact the gym owner about them.

## 11. Payments, refunds & test mode

Online payments and refunds are processed by a third-party payment gateway (Cashfree). When gateway keys are not
configured, **payments run in TEST MODE**: transactions are simulated, clearly labelled and no real money moves.
The page at `/terms` states whether the installation you are using is currently in test mode. The owner, the
developer and the AI are not responsible for failed, duplicated, delayed or disputed payments or refunds; resolve
them with the gym and the payment provider. Every incoming and outgoing transaction is logged for the gym's records.

## 12. Staff, payroll & tax

Salaries are calculated by the days worked. Payroll, gratuity, tax-withholding and settlement figures are
**estimates** based on simplified rules for each country and may be wrong or out of date. They are not tax or legal
advice. Employers must verify every amount with a qualified accountant and remain solely responsible for paying
staff correctly and on time (with proof of payment), and for complying with employment and international-payment
law. Employees can be removed only after their salary to date has been settled.

**Staff conduct, removal and bans.** A gym ends an employment by choosing a reason from a fixed list, and the reason
decides what happens next:

- **Reasons that let you rejoin** - a position change, a contract that ended, resignation, the role no longer being
  needed (restructuring) or relocation. You may be added again later, by the same gym or any other. A position (role
  or job title) is never changed in place: the old employment ends as a position change and you are invited to the
  new one, with the checks that role needs.
- **Conduct violations** - a behavioural issue, a policy violation, repeated no-shows or unprofessional conduct with
  members. The gym must describe what happened in a short note; the gym owner's gyms can never add you again, and a
  record is kept for the IronPulse platform team. Such removals by two different gym businesses within 12 months
  disable your IronPulse account automatically.
- **Severe violations** - harassment, violence or threats, theft or fraud, sexual misconduct, or endangering members'
  safety. Your IronPulse account is disabled at once, the public network addresses you used in the last 30 days are
  blocked for 30 days (an address shared by many people, such as a gym's Wi-Fi, is never blocked automatically) and
  the platform team is alerted.

A disabled account can't sign in, every device is signed out and its e-mail address, phone number and ID can't be
used for a new account. Removal reasons and notes are seen only by the gym that wrote them and the platform team -
never by other gyms, which are told only that they can't add the person. If you believe a removal or a ban is wrong,
reply to the e-mail you received: the platform team reviews it and can lift the ban.

## 16. Cookies & sessions

IronPulse uses only **essential cookies**: a session cookie (to keep you signed in), a security cookie that protects

your password signs you out everywhere. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

## 17. Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time. Deleted accounts are **kept for 30 days and then permanently erased**.
During those 30 days your username stays reserved and your name appears as "Deleted user" in shared history such as
chats. Financial records (payments, refunds, payroll) are kept in anonymised form where the law requires it.

**One person, one account - forever.** IronPulse allows one account per person. When an account is deleted (by you or
by an administrator) or disabled for a conduct violation, its e-mail address, phone number and ID number(s) can
**never be used to create a new account** - not even after the 30 days. To enforce this, IronPulse keeps only a keyed
hash of each (a one-way fingerprint computed with a secret key held by the platform), never the e-mail, number or ID
itself: the hash can tell that the same identifier is used again, but can't be turned back into it, and without the
secret key nobody can test guesses against it. These fingerprints are kept indefinitely so the rule keeps working;
they contain no other personal data. The deletion page asks you to confirm that you understand this.
