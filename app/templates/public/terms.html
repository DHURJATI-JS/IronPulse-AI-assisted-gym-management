By creating an account or using the site you agree to these Terms. If you don't agree, please don't use IronPulse.

Gyms listed on IronPulse are independent businesses. Each gym owner is solely responsible for their premises, equipment, staff, prices, classes, shop products and the services they provide. IronPulse does not operate any gym or shop.

4. Health & safety

Exercise carries a risk of injury. Consult a doctor before starting any training programme, especially if you have a health condition, an injury, are pregnant or take medication. Stop immediately and seek medical help if you feel pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or shortness of breath.

IronPulse can flag unusual entries - for example a sudden change in the weight or height you log, or symptoms you report in a wellness check or to the voice coach - and may pause your gym activities until a gym admin reviews them. IronPulse does not measure your heart rate or blood pressure. These checks are simple rules and AI estimates: they can miss problems and they can raise false alarms. They are not a diagnosis and do not replace a healthcare professional. In an emergency, call your local emergency services.

6. Live voice coach

  • The live coach talks with you during a workout. Speech recognition is done by your browser's speech service (for example Google in Chrome or Apple in Safari) under that provider's terms. IronPulse never records or stores your audio. You can turn the microphone off at any time and type instead.
  • The coach's voice is generated by a self-hosted text-to-speech engine (Kokoro) or, if that is unavailable, by your device's built-in voice.
  • You can ask the coach to pause, quit, change the exercise or the intensity. If you report chest pain, trouble breathing, fainting or similar symptoms, the session stops immediately, emergency guidance is shown, your gym activities may be paused and your gym (and guardian, for minors) may be notified. The coach is not a medical service - call emergency services if you feel unwell.
  • The animated face and its emotions are a visual aid; they don't reflect any assessment of you.

7. Protective gear suggestions

Based on the conditions you record, IronPulse may suggest protective gear - for example a back support strap or lifting belt for back problems, gloves for hand, grip or skin problems, or wrist wraps and knee sleeves. These are general suggestions, not medical prescriptions. Some gear isn't suitable for every condition (for example lifting belts with a hernia or during pregnancy). Ask your doctor or physiotherapist, and follow the manufacturer's instructions.
