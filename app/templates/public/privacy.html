IronPulse is a student-grade project. This notice explains, in plain language, what the app stores and why. It is not legal advice; see the Terms & disclaimer.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, e-mail, phone, date of birth, password (stored only as an Argon2id hash), time zone and city.
  • Guardian details for members under 18 (name, phone, e-mail, relation).
  • Gym data entered by owners: branches, addresses, opening hours, photos, registration and safety documents, plans and prices.
  • Staff data: role, photo, documents, shifts, salary and payout details (payout details are encrypted).
  • Activity: memberships, payments ledger, chat messages, tasks, reminders and notifications.
  • Job enquiries sent through the jobs board (name, e-mail, phone, message, optional résumé - stored encrypted).
  • Security logs: IP address and request metadata for rate limiting, attack detection and bans.

ID cards & photos

Every account except the platform administrator uploads a government ID card. ID images are encrypted at rest and only ever shown through authorised pages; ID numbers are stored masked plus a one-way hash used to detect the same ID on several accounts. Several OCR engines read the card to check it. Face photos used for presence or identity checks are never stored. Profile photos of gym owners and staff are public because members need to recognise them.

Location

We only ask for your device location when you tap “Use my location” (or similar). It's used to sort gyms by distance and to apply the {{ offline_km }} km rule for offline memberships, and - at the branch desk - to confirm you're within {{ presence_km }} km of the branch. If you're signed in you can save it to your account; otherwise it lives only in the page address. You can always use your city instead.

Health data

If you choose to enter health conditions, allergies, injuries or readings (such as heart rate), they're used to tailor plans and to raise safety alerts. Gym admins of gyms you're a member of may see alerts that need their review. This is sensitive data: only share what you're comfortable with.

AI processing

AI features send the minimum context needed (for example your goal, level and conditions) to an AI provider: Gemma via the project's gateway or a local Docker container, and Claude only as a fallback. Outputs are advice and are re-checked by rules. Images sent for document checks are downscaled and stripped of metadata first.

Who sees what

  • The public sees published branches, gym details, plans, and the names, photos and roles of public staff.
  • Gym owners see data of their own members, staff and job applicants - never other gyms' data.
  • Trainers see the clients assigned to them.
  • The platform administrator can review security events, verification queues and conversations to keep people safe.
  • We don't sell your data. E-mails go through the configured mail server; payments through the payment gateway.

Security

Strict content-security policy, CSRF protection, rate limits, attack detection, Argon2id password hashing and encryption at rest for private files. Uploaded images are re-encoded to strip hidden metadata (including GPS). No system is perfectly secure - see the disclaimer.

Retention & deletion

When you delete your account it's kept for {{ retention_days }} days and then erased, along with your files. Payment records keep an anonymised label so gyms' books stay correct. One person, one account - forever: the e-mail address, phone number and ID number(s) of a deleted account (or one disabled for a conduct violation) can never open a new account. For that, IronPulse keeps only a keyed hash of each - a one-way fingerprint made with a secret key, never the e-mail, number or ID itself - indefinitely; it contains no other personal data (Terms, section 17).

Your rights

  • See and correct your details on your account page.
  • Delete your account at any time (with the {{ retention_days }}-day window above).
  • Ask what we store about you or object to processing by contacting the administrator.
  • Guardians can contact us about a minor's account.
